# Credits

## The original games

**Scouts vs Aliens** and **Pedro Kicks Back** were created by **Hamumu Software** (copyright 2012): the game design, code, artwork and characters are theirs. Find their games at [hamumu.com](http://hamumu.com). The originals credit the music to **Nosoapradio.us**; the calculator ports have no music.

These ports are an unofficial fan project. They are not affiliated with or endorsed by Hamumu Software, and they grant no rights to the original games, artwork or music, which remain with their creators. The recovered original scripts and images kept under `original/` and `assets/` are reference material from the supplied games.

## Tools and libraries

- [CE C/C++ Toolchain](https://github.com/CE-Programming/toolchain) and [CE Libraries](https://github.com/CE-Programming/libraries) (graphx, keypadc, fileioc) for building and running on the TI-84 Plus CE.
- [CEmu](https://github.com/CE-Dev/CEmu) for emulator testing.
- [JPEXS Free Flash Decompiler](https://github.com/jindrapetrik/jpexs-decompiler) for recovering the original ActionScript and images.
- [Ruffle](https://ruffle.rs) for running the original games as a reference.
- Python and [Pillow](https://python-pillow.org) for asset conversion.
